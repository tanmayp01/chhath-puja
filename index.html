        What is Chhath?

        Chhath is a four-day festival of thanks to Surya Dev, the Sun, and to Chhathi Maiya, who is prayed to for the health and long life of children and family. It falls on Kartik Shukla Shashthi, six days after Diwali.

        It is the great festival of Bihar, Jharkhand, eastern Uttar Pradesh and Nepal's Madhesh, and today it lights up riverbanks, ponds and even rooftop tanks from Delhi and Mumbai to Mauritius, Dubai and New Jersey.

        Chhath is rare in honouring the setting sun as well as the rising one. There is no priest and no idol: the vratin (the person keeping the fast) stands in water and offers arghya directly to the Sun.

        The four days in 2026

        13
        Nov
        Day 1 · Friday

        Nahay Khay

        A holy bath and a spotless home. The vratin eats one pure meal, traditionally kaddu-bhaat: bottle gourd, chana dal and rice cooked with ghee and rock salt.

        14
        Nov
        Day 2 · Saturday

        Kharna

        A day-long fast, broken at dusk with rasiya (jaggery kheer) and roti cooked on a clay chulha with mango wood. After this prasad, the 36-hour nirjala fast begins, with not even water.

        15
        Nov
        Day 3 · Sunday

        Sandhya Arghya

        Thekua is fried at home, the daura is packed with fruit, and the family walks to the ghat singing. At sunset the vratin stands in the water and offers arghya to the setting sun. At night comes kosi bharai: diyas lit beneath a canopy of sugarcane.

        16
        Nov
        Day 4 · Monday

        Usha Arghya & Paran

        Back at the ghat before first light. Arghya is offered to the rising sun, prasad is shared with everyone, and the vratin breaks the fast (paran).

        What goes in the soop

        • Thekua: wheat flour, jaggery and ghee, pressed with a wooden mould and fried
        • Kasar or ladua: rice-flour and jaggery laddoos
        • Whole sugarcane, a bunch of bananas and a coconut
        • Big gagal nimbu (citron), sweet lime and singhara (water chestnut)
        • Fresh ginger and turmeric plants, radish and seasonal produce
        • A diya, sindoor and a lota of water or milk for the arghya

        Far from home this Chhath?

        • Find a local ghat: many cities set up Chhath ponds in parks and on lakes. Ask the Bihari or Purvanchali samaj near you.
        • Video-call family during both arghyas. Seeing the ghat live is the next best thing.
        • Make thekua at home: atta, gur, ghee, a pinch of saunf, and fry slowly on low flame.
        • Play the geet. Our songs panel has Sharda Sinha ji's classics.

        Ghat etiquette

        • Give vratins space at the water's edge; they have not had water for a day and a half.
        • Keep the river clean. Carry back plastic and leave only flowers and diyas.
        • Never step over a soop, daura or prasad.
        • Watch children near the water and stay inside the barricades.
        • Accept prasad with both hands, and offer it to whoever is near you.

        Chhath Puja FAQ

        When is Chhath Puja in 2026?

        Chhath Puja 2026 runs from Friday 13 November to Monday 16 November 2026: Nahay Khay on 13 Nov, Kharna on 14 Nov, Sandhya Arghya on Sunday 15 Nov and Usha Arghya on Monday 16 Nov.

        What is the Sandhya Arghya and Usha Arghya time in 2026?

        Sandhya Arghya on 15 Nov 2026 is at sunset: about 5:01 PM in Patna, 5:28 PM in Delhi and 6:00 PM in Mumbai. Usha Arghya on 16 Nov 2026 is at sunrise: about 6:07 AM in Patna, 6:44 AM in Delhi and 6:46 AM in Mumbai. Check your local panchang.

        What are the four days of Chhath Puja?

        Day 1 is Nahay Khay (holy bath and a meal of kaddu-bhaat), Day 2 is Kharna (a day-long fast broken with rasiya kheer, after which the 36-hour nirjala fast begins), Day 3 is Sandhya Arghya (offering to the setting sun and kosi bharai at night), and Day 4 is Usha Arghya and Paran (offering to the rising sun and breaking the fast).

        How can I celebrate Chhath if I can't go home?

        Chhath Ghat Online lets you live all four days of Chhath on a 3D ghat in your browser: take the holy dip, cook kharna kheer and thekua, offer arghya at sunset and sunrise, light the kosi and float diyas, with Sharda Sinha's Chhath geet playing. You can also find a local Chhath ghat in your city and video-call family during the arghya.

        What goes in the Chhath soop and daura?

        Thekua, kasar or ladua, whole sugarcane, bananas, coconut, gagal nimbu (citron), sweet lime, singhara, fresh ginger and turmeric plants, a diya, sindoor and a lota of water or milk for the arghya.

        Which are the most famous Chhath songs?

        Sharda Sinha's Chhath geet are the soundtrack of the festival, including Kelwa Ke Paat Par, Pahile Pahil Chhathi Maiya, Ho Deenanath, Uthau Suruj Bhaile Bihaan, Kartik Mahinwan Ke and Ugihein Sooraj Gosaiyan Hey, along with Kaanch Hi Baans Ke Bahangiya.
